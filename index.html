FAQ

Got Questions?
We Got Answers.

What services does AFX Studio offer?
We offer digital marketing, social media marketing, content creation (reels, videos, photography), Meta ads campaigns, website development, e-commerce development, and SaaS/app development — all under one roof.
How long does a website project take?
A standard website takes 2–4 weeks. E-commerce stores typically take 3–6 weeks depending on complexity. SaaS/app projects are scoped individually and usually range from 2–6 months.
Do you work with small businesses?
Absolutely. We work with businesses of all sizes — from local startups to established brands. Our packages are flexible and tailored to your budget and goals.
How do Meta Ads campaigns work?
We handle everything — from audience research and creative production to campaign setup, A/B testing, and weekly performance reporting. You get full visibility into spend and results.
Can you manage my social media monthly?
Yes! We offer monthly social media management packages that include content planning, shooting, editing, posting, and engagement management.
Where are you based?
We are based in South Tamil Nadu, India. We serve clients locally across Tamil Nadu as well as remotely across India and internationally.
